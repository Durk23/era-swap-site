Era Swap

Two generations, one game.

You answer quick questions about the other person’s childhood. A grandma who grew up in the ’60s gets 2010s questions, and her grandkid gets ’60s questions. After every round, Story Time shows what each of you missed, so you can tell each other what it was really like.

  • Family duel: pass one phone back and forth, five questions each.
  • Family online: challenge family anywhere and play your turns when you have a minute.
  • Ghost race: race a saved round from family or friends at the speed they really played, or a practice ghost from any decade.
  • Open match: play someone from another generation, with made-up names and no chat.
