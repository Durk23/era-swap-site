Era Swap

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version

  • Family duel works entirely on your device. Nothing about it leaves your phone.
  • Online play is for ages 13 and up. It uses Apple’s iCloud to share the first name or nickname you choose, the decade you grew up in, and your game results with family and friends you connect with by code.
  • Strangers in Open match only ever see a made-up name like “Snazzy Otter 42.” There is no chat anywhere in the app.
  • No ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your information. We never ask for your email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

Who we are

Era Swap is made by Mike Durkin-Sztukowski (“we” or “us”). You can reach us at mike@durkingrowthsolutions.com.

What stays on your device

Most of what Era Swap knows never leaves your phone or tablet:

  • The first names or nicknames and childhood decades of the people who play on it
  • Scores, saved rounds (“ghosts”), head-to-head records, and which questions each player has already seen
  • Your settings, your list of blocked players, and any online turn you haven’t finished

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What online play shares

Online play (Family online, ghost races with family and friends, and Open match) is optional and for ages 13 and up. The first time you go online, the app asks your age. It keeps only whether you’re 13 or older, never the age itself, and if you’re under 13, online play stays off on that device.

Online play also needs a one-tap Sign in with Apple. We don’t ask Apple for your name or email address. Apple gives the app an identifier for your account that is unique to our apps, and it stays on your device.

When you play online, Era Swap keeps the following in its database on Apple’s iCloud servers (Apple’s CloudKit service):

  • Your friend code, the first name or nickname you entered, and the decade you grew up in
  • Results from your last 10 rounds: which questions you got right, got with a clue, or missed, how long each answer took, and your points
  • For Family online games: the invite code, each player’s name and decade (including the name and decade the challenger enters for the person they invite), each player’s answers and scores, clue requests, which preset clue was sent back, and reminders (“nudges”)
  • When someone races a family member’s or friend’s saved round: the racer’s name and both scores, so the owner can see how it went

Who can see it. Your name or nickname, decade and scores are shown only to people you’re connected with by a code: family and friends who add you or whom you add, and the people in your Family online games. Because your profile is found by your friend code, anyone who has your code could see the same things, so we suggest using a first name or nickname rather than a full name.

iCloud keeps a random identifier for whoever created each record. It doesn’t tell us your Apple ID, name or email address. Apple stores this information for us; Apple’s Privacy Policy describes how Apple handles it.

Open match (playing someone new)

Open match uses Apple’s Game Center to find another player who grew up in a different decade.

  • Other players see only a generated name, like “Snazzy Otter 42,” and the decade you pick. They never see your real name, your Game Center nickname, a photo, your age or your location.
  • During a match, the app sends your opponent your results, answer times, points and any reactions you tap, through Game Center. It also sends the results of your most recent round, so the match can finish fairly if one of you leaves.
  • There is no chat. Players can only send preset reactions.
  • If you report a player, we keep the reason you picked, their Game Center player identifier and generated name, your friend code, and the time, so we can review the report. Other players can’t read reports.
  • Blocking a player saves their Game Center player identifier on your device so you’re never matched with them again.

Open match isn’t available to Game Center accounts that Apple marks as belonging to a child, or that are restricted from playing with others.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple’s push notification service delivers messages such as “Grandma Jo just played. Your move.” These include the other player’s first name or nickname. You can turn notifications off anytime in your device’s Settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for the Single Pass and the Family Pass, and we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple which pass is active for your account or, for the Family Pass, your Family Sharing group.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party code in the app
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information
  • We don’t access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location

Children

Era Swap is made for families, and kids often play with parents and grandparents. Family duel works entirely on the device, so kids of any age can play it without sharing anything.

Online play is for ages 13 and up. The app asks each player’s age before they go online for the first time and keeps online play off for anyone under 13. Kids under 13 can still play online with family on a parent’s or grandparent’s phone, under that grown-up’s account. Open match is also turned off for Game Center accounts that Apple identifies as belonging to children.

We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has shared information with us, email mike@durkingrowthsolutions.com and we’ll delete it.

How long we keep information

Information on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Online information stays in Era Swap’s iCloud database until you delete it in the app or ask us to delete it. Reports you file are kept as long as we need them to review them and keep Open match safe, unless you delete your online information first.

Your choices

  • Play Family duel without signing in or going online at all.
  • Change your name or decade, or sign out, in the app’s Settings.
  • Turn off reactions from other players, and unblock players, in Settings.
  • Delete your online information at any time: in the app, open Settings and tap “Delete my online information” under Online play. It deletes your friend code, name, decade, scores, the games you started, your turns, clues, nudges, ghost race results and the reports you filed, right away, and signs you out of online play. Games and scores saved on your device stay until you delete the app.
  • If someone else started a game with you, their game keeps the name and decade they typed in for you. Email mike@durkingrowthsolutions.com and we’ll delete those too, or anything else you’d like removed, within 30 days.
  • To disconnect Era Swap from your Apple ID as well, open your device’s Settings, tap your name, tap Sign in with Apple, choose Era Swap, then tap Delete.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
