Getting started

What is Era Swap?

A trivia game for two generations. You answer quick questions about the other person’s childhood decade: a grandma who grew up in the ’60s gets 2010s questions, and her grandkid gets ’60s questions. After each round, Story Time lists what each of you missed, so you can tell each other what it was really like.

How do we play on one phone?

Choose Family duel, enter both players’ names and when you were kids, and pass the phone back and forth. Family duel is always free and works without the internet.

How do we play when we’re far apart?

Choose Family online. Challenge someone from your Family and Friends list, or create an invite and send them the code. You each play your turn whenever you have a minute, and you both see the results and Story Time once the second turn is in.

How do I add family or friends?

Open the Friends tab. Share your friend code (it looks like ERA-7K3Q) and ask for theirs, then enter their name and code and choose Family or Friend. There’s no search, so people can only connect by code.

Why do I need to sign in?

Only the online modes need it. It’s a quick age question and one tap with Sign in with Apple, and we never see your email address. Family duel and races against practice ghosts work without signing in.

Playing

How does scoring work?

A right answer is worth 100 points, plus up to 50 more for answering quickly. With a clue it’s worth 50, plus the same speed bonus. A wrong answer or running out of time is worth 0. If the scores tie, the faster total time wins.

How do clues work?

In Family duel, ask the other player for a clue out loud; a clue also appears on screen. In Family online, asking sends the other player a notification, and they pick one of a few preset clues to send back. That question waits while you keep playing, and you finish it when the clue arrives.

What’s a ghost race?

Every round anyone plays becomes their “ghost.” Race a family member’s or friend’s ghost and their real answers and timing play back next to yours. The purple mark on the timer shows when they answered.

Can I play if I don’t have anyone to race?

Yes. Ghost race always has practice ghosts, one for each decade from the ’40s to the 2020s. They’re made-up players, like Grandma Bev from the ’50s, who answer at a realistic speed. Each one plays a new round every time you race, so a rematch is never a replay. The ’50s, ’80s and 2010s practice ghosts are free to race as often as you like, and either pass unlocks the other six. Practice races work without signing in or going online.

What is Open match?

A live game with someone new who grew up in a different decade. You both answer at the same time and see each other’s result after every question. Players see only made-up names, and there’s no chat, just preset reactions.

Safety

Can players chat or send messages?

No. Players can only send each other preset reactions and preset clues. The names you type are shown only to family and friends you connect with by code.

How do I report or block someone?

At the end of an Open match, tap “Report or block this player” and pick a reason, or choose “Just block them.” Blocked players are never matched with you again. You can unblock players in the app’s Settings.

Why does the app ask my age?

Online play is for ages 13 and up. The app asks everyone once, the first time they go online, and it doesn’t save the age itself.

Can kids under 13 play online?

Yes, on a grown-up’s phone, using that grown-up’s account. Family duel works for everyone, on any phone, because it keeps everything on the device. Open match is also turned off for Game Center accounts that Apple marks as belonging to a child.

I picked the wrong age.

The answer stays until the app is deleted. To answer again, delete Era Swap and reinstall it from the App Store. Deleting the app also deletes the games saved on that phone.

Single Pass and Family Pass

What do the passes include?

Both unlock the same things: unlimited Family online turns, ghost races with family and friends, and Open matches, plus practice ghosts from every decade. Each is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and there are no ads either way. Without a pass, Family duel and the ’50s, ’80s and 2010s practice ghosts are always free, plus 1 online round a day.

What’s the difference?

Who they cover. The Single Pass (US$5.99 in the United States) works on every iPhone and iPad signed in with the Apple ID that bought it. The Family Pass (US$13.99) also works for up to five other people in your Apple Family Sharing group, on their own devices.

How does the Family Pass reach my family?

Through Apple’s Family Sharing. One grown-up sets up a family group and invites up to five other people. They don’t have to live together, but each person can only be in one family group at a time. Make sure Purchase Sharing is turned on: open your device’s Settings, tap your name, then Family Sharing. Once anyone in the group buys the Family Pass, everyone else gets it on their own devices.

I have the Single Pass. Can my family use it?

No. The Single Pass works only for the Apple ID that bought it. To cover your family, get the Family Pass, which is a separate purchase.

I bought a pass, but it isn’t unlocked.

Open the app’s Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase. For the Family Pass, an Apple ID in the same Family Sharing group works too.

Trouble and questions

I’m not getting notifications.

Open your device’s Settings, tap Notifications, then Era Swap, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

How do I delete my information?

In the app, open Settings and tap “Delete my online information” under Online play. It deletes your friend code, name, scores, online games and everything else you shared online, right away, and signs you out of online play. Games and scores saved on your phone stay until you delete the app. You can also email us your friend code, and we’ll delete your online information within 30 days.
